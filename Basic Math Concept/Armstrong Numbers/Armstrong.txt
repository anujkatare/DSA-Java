Armstrong numbers (also called Narcissistic numbers) are numbers that are equal to the sum of their own digits raised to the power of the number of digits.

Definition (simple words)

A number is an Armstrong number if
sum of (each digit ^ total digits) = the number itself

EXAMPLE:

Digits: 1, 5, 3
Number of digits = 3

1³ + 5³ + 3³
= 1 + 125 + 27
= 153 ✅
